## Lifecycle ordering discrepancy and correction (T027 complete; T008/T028 identity gate open)

Probe 02 produced `valid=false complete=false` because segment 164's restore arrived after the terminal record. Probe 05 then appeared to validate the fix, but its retained event log places terminal before the final suspend record for segment 335. The ledger had removed that segment from its active set before the suspend event sink ran, allowing `complete=true` while the event stream was still incomplete. See `artifacts/f062-filtered-rp-probe-05-ordering-review.md`.

The correction adds an explicit closing-segment state. `beginSegmentRestore` atomically moves a segment from active to closing; `CoroutineTraceLifecycle` emits the suspend marker; `finishSegmentRestore` marks it closed and allows deferred terminal emission only after both active and closing sets are empty. Host test `terminal follows the final suspend marker when recorded during restore` covers the race. Probe 08 confirms this order on device. No measured lookup boundary is extended and no segment is suppressed. T027 is complete. T008/T028 remain open until the new source/APK identity is calibrated.
