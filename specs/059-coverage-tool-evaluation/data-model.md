# Evidence Model: Coverage Tool Evaluation

## Candidate

Name, version, engine and front-end relationship; configurations, source/workload scope, and inclusion rationale. Exactly JaCoCo and Kover are required, with at most one additional credible candidate. A configuration is not an extra candidate.

## Workload inventory

Revision, policy and toolchain identity; configured modules, source sets, variants, required host and device tasks, exclusions, runtime and device requirements. Distinguish required, optional and excluded workloads.

## Capability assessment

Candidate and version, dimension, workload and variant scope, disposition (supported, unsupported or unverified), evidence references, limitations and date. Every material candidate/dimension pair must have a disposition; unverified is not a pass.

## Evidence and measurement

Source identity, version, retrieval date and location or receipt; evidence type (documented, measured, historical), supported claim and limitations. Measurements additionally identify revision, commands, environment, tests, inventory, variant, tool versions, raw covered and missed instruction/branch counts, artifacts, and comparability limitations. No numerical equivalence follows from matching format alone.

## Cost observation

Candidate, cost dimension, scope, basis (measured, documented or unknown), units and conditions where known; avoid estimated savings without an evidenced baseline.

## Recommendation

Retain, replace or defer; reasons linked to assessments, trade-offs, unknowns, and evidence limits; separately approved follow-up where relevant. A recommendation is recorded only after required dimensions have dispositions, including explicit unknowns. A replacement recommendation itself does not authorize migration.
