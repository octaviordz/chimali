## Summary

Investigate and document the warm-08 FIDO2 security-key registration failure observed on Android 15 (ASUS_AI2302) by building deterministic host-side behavioral test harnesses. The investigation focuses on testing two primary defect hypotheses identified in static analysis:
1. **Executor Leak**: `Executors.newSingleThreadExecutor()` allocated per `registerApp()` call without shutdown, accumulating orphaned background threads.
2. **Callback Race Hazard**: Out-of-order delivery of `onAppStatusChanged(registered=false)` from a prior asynchronous `unregisterApp()` interfering with an active registration attempt's continuation.

Per **Backlog §5** and **FR-006**, **NO production code changes** will be made during this investigation phase. The deliverable is a comprehensive behavioral verification suite that isolates whether these defects cause the failure or eliminates them from suspicion.
