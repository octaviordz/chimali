## Current host behavior coverage

- The Android adapter sets the sensitive flag and fresh ownership token before calling `setPrimaryClip`, schedules one attempt at the fixed 30-second deadline, reads description metadata only, and clears only when the readable token matches. Cancellation from publication, metadata access or explicit clear is not converted to success or an ordinary error result. Cancellation of a scheduled attempt while the app remains active is recorded as `Interrupted` without claiming removal; abrupt process death cannot record a terminal result.
- The Vault caller shows cleanup as unconfirmed when the custodian reports `Interrupted`, then acknowledges that receipt without claiming removal.
- Different or unreadable metadata skips automatic clearing. An unconfirmed candidate leaves predecessor work scheduled; admission is refused while uncertainty is pending or receipt capacity is occupied.
- A failed explicit clear leaves automatic work pending. Error objects expose fixed messages and do not retain synthetic platform exception text or causes.
- Admitted receipt outcomes are retained until acknowledged, abandoned, or transferred to the custodian. The handoff and terminal disposition use a shared synchronized section; pending work remains scheduled after handoff.
- The Vault caller reports confirmed versus unconfirmed publication separately, consumes outcomes by generation, acknowledges terminal statuses and hands pending receipts off on permanent disposal. Admission refusal and explicit-clear failure use state-neutral, non-success wording.
- A replacement ViewModel starts without restored secret UI state. No test here proves Android process recreation, process death, or cleanup after process death.
